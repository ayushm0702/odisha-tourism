About Odisha

Odisha, on India's eastern coast, is known for its rich heritage, classical Odissi dance, Pattachitra art and magnificent Kalinga-style temples.

From the Jagannath Temple in Puri to the Sun Temple at Konark, from the vast Chilika Lake to the forests of Similipal, the state offers something for every traveller - pilgrims, history lovers, nature seekers and food explorers.

Capital: Bhubaneswar  |  Language: Odia  |  Best time to visit: October - March

Quick Facts

  • Called the "Temple City" - Bhubaneswar
  • Home of Odissi classical dance
  • Chilika - Asia's largest brackish water lagoon
  • Famous for Pattachitra & Sambalpuri textiles

Top Destinations

Click any place name to read more on Wikipedia.

🌊

Puri

Pilgrimage · Beach

Home to a lively beach and one of the Char Dham pilgrimage sites, famous for the annual Rath Yatra.

🛕

Jagannath Temple, Puri

Temple · Pilgrimage

The 12th-century Kalinga-style temple dedicated to Lord Jagannath, the spiritual heart of Odisha.

☀️

Konark Sun Temple

Heritage · UNESCO

Designed as a giant stone chariot with intricately carved wheels, it is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

🏛️

Bhubaneswar

Temples · Culture

The state capital and "Temple City" with hundreds of ancient temples.

🛕

Lingaraj Temple

Temple · Architecture

One of the largest and oldest temples in Bhubaneswar, a masterpiece of Kalinga architecture.

⛰️

Udayagiri and Khandagiri Caves

History · Caves

Rock-cut caves near Bhubaneswar with ancient Jain inscriptions and carvings.

☸️

Dhauli

Buddhist · History

A hill with the Shanti Stupa and Ashoka's rock edicts, linked to the Kalinga War.

🦩

Chilika Lake

Nature · Birdwatching

A vast lagoon with islands, migratory birds in winter and chances to spot Irrawaddy dolphins.

🐅

Similipal National Park

Wildlife · Waterfalls

A tiger reserve with dense sal forests, elephants and Barehipani & Joranda falls.

🐊

Bhitarkanika National Park

Mangroves · Wildlife

Mangrove forests with saltwater crocodiles, near beaches where Olive Ridley turtles nest.

🏙️

Cuttack

City · Heritage

The former capital on the Mahanadi, known for silver filigree work and Bali Jatra.

🎨

Raghurajpur

Art · Village

A heritage craft village famed for Pattachitra paintings and palm-leaf engravings.

🏖️

Gopalpur-on-Sea

Beach · Relaxation

A calm and less crowded beach town on the Bay of Bengal.

🌅

Chandipur

Beach · Unique

A beach where the sea recedes several kilometres during low tide.

🌉

Hirakud Dam

Engineering · Lake

One of the world's longest earthen dams, built across the Mahanadi near Sambalpur.

🌄

Daringbadi

Hill Station · Nature

Often called the "Kashmir of Odisha", a cool hill station with coffee and pepper plantations.

🌿

Satkosia Tiger Reserve

Wildlife · Gorge

A scenic reserve around the Mahanadi gorge with forests, wildlife and river boating.

🧘

Ratnagiri, Odisha

Buddhist · Archaeology

A major ancient Buddhist site with monasteries and stupas in the Jajpur district.

🦁

Nandankanan Zoological Park

Zoo · Family

A zoo and botanical garden near Bhubaneswar known for its white tigers.

🏞️

Koraput

Tribal · Hills

A hilly district with tribal culture, waterfalls, coffee estates and scenic valleys.

🚣

Ansupa Lake

Lake · Picnic

A freshwater oxbow lake on the Mahanadi, popular for boating and bird watching.

Festivals & Culture

Rath Yatra

June / July - Puri

Lord Jagannath, Balabhadra and Subhadra are pulled in giant chariots by lakhs of devotees.

Konark Dance Festival

December - Konark

Classical dancers perform against the backdrop of the Sun Temple.

Raja Parba

June - Statewide

A unique three-day festival celebrating womanhood and the arrival of monsoon.

Bali Jatra

Kartika Purnima - Cuttack

Commemorates Odisha's ancient maritime trade with Southeast Asia.
